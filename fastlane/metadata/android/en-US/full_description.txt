WikiTok turns Wikipedia into an endless, swipeable vertical feed — the TikTok interaction model pointed at the sum of human knowledge instead of dances.

Features:

• Endless feed of Wikipedia articles with full-bleed images; articles without an image get generated gradient art
• On-device recommender: the more you like, read, and expand, the better the feed matches your interests. The embedding model is trained on Wikipedia itself (source and training pipeline in the repo) and runs in pure Kotlin; your profile is 96 floats in a local file. Nothing is ever downloaded and nothing leaves your device — there is no server.
• Mix multiple Wikipedia language editions in one feed, interleaved card by card
• Articles with Wikimedia Commons videos autoplay instantly, muted and looping
• Wikipedia's featured article of the day, badged in the feed
• Twice-daily digest notification with an article picked for you
• Tap to expand the extract, double-tap to like, long-press to share
• Save articles for later; open the full article on Wikipedia any time

Free and open source. Content: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA); this app is an unofficial reader and is not affiliated with the Wikimedia Foundation.
